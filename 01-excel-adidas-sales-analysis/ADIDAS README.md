# Adidas US Sales Analysis — Excel Dashboard

## Project Overview

This project analyses US retail sales data using Microsoft Excel. I developed a sales performance dashboard to explore product performance, regional sales, sales methods, monthly trends, profitability, and data quality.

## Dashboard Preview

![Adidas US Sales Dashboard](./ADIDAS%20dashboard1.png)

## Objectives

- Compare recorded sales and operating profit across products.
- Analyse sales and profitability by region.
- Explore monthly sales and operating profit trends.
- Compare performance across sales methods.
- Identify potential inconsistencies in the source data.

## Tools and Skills

- Microsoft Excel
- PivotTables and PivotCharts
- Excel formulas and data validation
- KPI cards and dashboard design
- Data visualisation and business insights.

## Key Analysis Areas

**Product Performance:** Compared recorded sales and operating profit across six product categories.

**Regional Performance:** Compared recorded sales and operating profit across five regions.

**Monthly Sales Trend:** Visualised monthly changes in recorded sales and operating profit.

**Sales Method Analysis:** Compared recorded sales across Online, Outlet, and In-store methods.

**Data Quality:** Checked whether recorded Total Sales matched Price per Unit multiplied by Units Sold.

## Key Data Quality Finding

The validation check identified **3,886 of 9,648 records (40.28%)** where recorded Total Sales equals exactly 10 times Price per Unit multiplied by Units Sold.

This pattern requires further investigation. The recorded values have not been automatically changed or assumed to be incorrect. Sales and profit findings should be interpreted with this limitation in mind.
